# GENZ GADGETS

## Smart Shopkeeper Inventory & Stock Management System

GENZ GADGETS is a web-based inventory and stock management system developed for electronics and gadget shops. The application provides a centralized platform to manage products, customers, suppliers, purchases, sales, stock, and reports.

The system is designed to simplify day-to-day shop management and reduce the need for manual inventory records.

---

## Features

- Admin login and authentication
- Dashboard with inventory information
- Product management
- Add, edit, view, and delete products
- Stock quantity management
- Customer management
- Supplier management
- Purchase management
- Sales management
- Automatic stock updates
- Search functionality
- Input validation
- Low-stock monitoring
- Inventory value tracking
- Reports and business information

---

## Main Modules

### Dashboard

The dashboard provides an overview of the shop's inventory and important stock information.

### Inventory Management

The inventory module allows the shopkeeper to:

- Add new products
- View available products
- Edit product information
- Delete products
- Update stock quantities
- Search for products
- Monitor stock levels

### Customer Management

The customer module stores and manages:

- Customer name
- Phone number
- Email address
- Address

### Supplier Management

The supplier module manages:

- Company name
- Contact person
- Phone number
- Email address
- Address

### Purchase Management

The purchase module records products purchased from suppliers and updates the available stock.

### Sales Management

The sales module records products sold to customers and updates the remaining stock.

The system also checks the available stock before processing a sale.

### Reports

The reports module provides useful information about inventory and business activities.
